# Reviewing Pull Requests in AITER

> **DRAFT** — this page is being trialed and may change. Feedback is welcome in an issue.

AITER welcomes help with code review from everyone, including contributors outside the core team.

## Roles

- **Area contact**: the first point of contact for an area. Makes sure each pull request in the area gets a
  reviewer. Listed in [CONTACTS.md](CONTACTS.md).
- **Reviewer**: anyone who reviews pull requests and gives technical feedback.
- **Maintainer**: a member of the AITER team with write access. A maintainer's approval is required to merge.

## How to start

You do not need any special status to review. Pick a pull request in an area you know, read the change, try it
if you can, and leave comments. Useful reviews point out correctness issues, missing tests, performance
regressions and unclear code, and they explain why.

Approvals from reviewers outside the AITER team are welcome as feedback, but they do not replace a maintainer's
approval for merging.

## Community reviewers

Over time, the maintainers may invite contributors who consistently give helpful reviews in an area to be
listed as reviewers for that area in [CONTACTS.md](CONTACTS.md).

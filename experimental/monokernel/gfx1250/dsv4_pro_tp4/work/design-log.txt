Candidate design (pending profile):
Retain stage 1 split-K mHC projection and stage 2 reduce/Sinkhorn/RMSNorm.
Extend stage 2 to emit FP8 input and column-major per-128 E8M0 scales for wqkv_a.
BF16 normalized output remains materialized for compressor/indexer consumers.
Ownership: each stage-1 CTA owns a row tile/K-split and writes partial sums and
residual region; kernel completion orders stage 2 reads. Each stage-2 CTA owns
whole output rows; within-row group max reductions create scales without global
flags. FP8 payload, scale and BF16 output all become ready at kernel completion.
No added cross-rank or cross-CTA synchronization. Existing MoE and collectives stay.
Resource validation needed: stage-2 registers/spills/LDS compared to baseline;
FP8 group reductions should reuse already-normalized register values.
Numerical contract: normalize exactly in baseline order, round to BF16 first,
then per-128 FP8 e4m3fn quantization with HIP RoundUp E8M0 policy (NOT the old
Gluon common mx_e8m0_scale helper's round-even policy). Verify exact scale bytes,
FP8 payload and original outputs against original mHC + per_group_quant_hip.

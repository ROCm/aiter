Implementation: mHC+FP8 quant fusion through16 rows; exact unsplit Gluon attention and shared-expert projections at1/8 rows; C1 routed FP4 MoE gate/up+SwiGLU+MXFP8 quant+down+ordered reduction fused into one192-CTA launch. Larger rows retain library routed MoE. Router/input quant/shared expert remain separate. This is regional hybrid fusion, not a full-layer monokernel.

Validation: all43 real MoE layers passed exact comparisons;249 requests completed across1/8/16/32/64/128. Each selected projection logged256/640 exact comparisons. Isolated MoE tests include1000 changing-input graph replays,uint32 wrap,random scatter and uint16 routing. Intermediate payloads use DEV-scoped TDM/buffer operations,explicit store drains and relaxed GPU semaphores.
